Deleting your LocalUp account

Last updated 7 September 2026

You can delete your LocalUp account and everything in it yourself, from inside the app, at any time. You do not need to ask us.

From inside the app

  1. Open the Profile tab.
  2. Tap the settings icon in the top right.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Deletion is immediate and permanent. There is no recovery window and no grace period. If you sign up again later it is a new account with nothing carried over.

If you no longer have the app installed

Email [SUPPORT EMAIL] from the address on the account. We will delete it and confirm to you within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in credentials
  • Your profile — name, date of birth, home city, bio, interests, and any politics or religion you chose to fill in
  • Every photo you uploaded, removed from storage as well as from the app
  • Your matches, and every message in them
  • Your notification tokens and your distance and age filters

What is kept, and for how long

Nothing that identifies you. A message you sent is removed along with the match it belongs to, on both sides. Beyond that, our hosting provider keeps routine encrypted backups of the database as a whole; your data ages out of those within 30 days and they are never used to restore an individual account.
