LocalUp

Meet locals where you're travelling — or show your city to someone passing through.

LocalUp is an Android app that introduces travellers to the people who actually live in the city they are visiting. Tell it where you live, pick the things you care about, and it puts the people who share them in front of you. When you both say yes, the conversation opens.

It is free, carries no advertising, and has nothing to buy. It is for adults, 18 and over.
