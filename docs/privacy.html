Privacy Policy

LocalUp · Last updated 7 September 2026

LocalUp connects travellers with locals in the same city. This policy explains exactly what the app stores, why, and who else ever sees it. It describes what the app actually does, not what an app like it might do.

Who we are

LocalUp is developed and operated by an individual developer. For any question about your data, or to exercise any right below, write to [SUPPORT EMAIL].

What we collect, and why

  • Account — your email address and a password, or your Google account if you choose to sign in that way. Used only to authenticate you.
  • Profile — display name, date of birth, home city and its coordinates, a short bio, and your chosen interests. This is what other users see and what decides who you are shown to.
  • Age — we store your date of birth and display only your age. It confirms you are over 18 and drives age filtering.
  • Photos — images you upload from your device's photo library. We never access your camera, and we never read your library: you pick each image yourself.
  • Approximate location — your city-level position, so the app can tell whether you are local or travelling and show you people nearby. We ask when we need it and never collect it in the background. Your precise position is never shown to another user.
  • Politics and religion — optional. See the section below; these are treated differently from everything else here.
  • Messages — text you send to people you have matched with, stored so both of you can read the conversation.
  • Notification token — an anonymous push identifier issued by Expo, plus whether your device is Android or iOS. It carries no device identifier, no advertising ID and no phone number.
  • Preferences — your distance and age filters.

Politics and religion

Under the GDPR these are special category data (Article 9), alongside things like health and sexual orientation. We only process them with your explicit consent, which you give by choosing an answer and withdraw by removing it.

Both fields are entirely optional. No screen requires them, and nothing in the app is unavailable if you leave them blank.

They are used for one purpose only: the order in which people appear in your deck. They are never used to filter — nobody is hidden from you, and you are hidden from nobody, because of an answer to either question.

Leaving them blank costs you nothing. Our ranking scores an unanswered question neutrally, in the middle of the range — never at the bottom. We do not want anyone pressured into disclosing a belief in order to be seen.

To withdraw: open Edit profile → Beliefs and tap your answer again. It is removed immediately, not when you next press Save.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your data.
  • We show no advertising and carry no advertising or analytics SDK.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We collect no payment information — the app is free and has nothing to buy.
  • We do not collect contacts, calendar, call logs, SMS, files or audio.

Who processes data on our behalf

  • Supabase — hosts the database, authentication and photo storage. Everything listed above is stored there.
  • Expo — delivers push notifications. Receives only the notification token and the message we send.
  • Google — only if you choose Google sign-in, and only to confirm who you are.
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim) — turns a city name into coordinates and coordinates into a city name. It receives the city text you type and, on the location step, your approximate coordinates. It receives nothing that identifies you.

Who can see what

Other users see your display name, age, photos, bio, interests, home city, whether you are currently local or travelling, and any belief you chose to fill in. They never see your email address, your date of birth, or your precise coordinates. Messages are visible only to the two people in a match.

How long we keep it

We keep your data until you delete your account. Deleting removes your profile, photos, interests, matches, messages and notification tokens permanently. You can do this yourself from Settings inside the app; the process is described at the account deletion page.

Your rights

If you are in the EU or the UK, the GDPR gives you the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, to withdraw consent, and to object to processing. Most of this is already in the app: your profile is editable in Edit profile, beliefs are removable with one tap, and deletion is in Settings. For anything else, write to [SUPPORT EMAIL] and we will respond within 30 days.

Age

LocalUp is for adults. You must be 18 or older, and the app refuses a date of birth that would make you younger.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and data is encrypted at rest by our hosting provider. Access is enforced at the database level with row-level security, so one account cannot read another's private data.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and notify you in the app.
