Community Guidelines

LocalUp · Last updated 7 September 2026

LocalUp exists so that people visiting a city and people who live in it can meet each other. These rules are what keep that worth doing. Breaking them gets an account removed.

You must be 18 or older

LocalUp is for adults only. The app requires a date of birth and refuses anyone under 18. Creating an account by giving a false date of birth is a breach of these guidelines and the account will be removed when we find out.

Child safety — zero tolerance

Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) is absolutely prohibited on LocalUp.

This includes Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), any sexualisation of a minor, grooming, sextortion, trafficking, and any other exploitation or endangerment of a child. There are no exceptions and no warnings.

Accounts found engaging in any of the above are removed immediately and permanently, and their content is deleted.

Where we obtain actual knowledge of CSAM, we remove it and report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), or to the relevant authority in the reporting party's jurisdiction, as required by applicable law.

To report CSAE or any child-safety concern on LocalUp, email localuprn@gmail.com with the word CHILD SAFETY in the subject line. Reports of this kind are prioritised above everything else.

The designated point of contact for child-safety matters, including notifications from Google Play, is localuprn@gmail.com.

Be a real person, and be yourself

  • Use photos that are actually of you.
  • Do not impersonate anyone else.
  • One person, one account.

Not allowed

  • Harassment — threats, abuse, or continuing to contact someone who has stopped replying.
  • Hate speech — attacking people over race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, disability, gender, age, or sexual orientation.
  • Sexual content — nudity or sexually explicit photos in profiles.
  • Violence — threatening it, glorifying it, or organising it.
  • Selling and soliciting — commercial sex, drugs, weapons, advertising, or promoting other services.
  • Scams — asking matches for money, investment schemes, phishing, or links to fraudulent sites.
  • Sharing private information — someone else's contact details, address, or photos, without their permission.

Tools you have

Every profile can be blocked, which is immediate and works in both directions: neither of you appears to the other again. You can unmatch at any time, which ends the conversation. To report a person or a message, email localuprn@gmail.com with their profile name and what happened.

Meeting people in person. LocalUp introduces you to strangers. Meet in a public place, tell someone where you are going, and leave whenever you want to. Trust your judgement over politeness.

What happens when a rule is broken

Depending on what was done, we remove the content, remove the account, or both. Child-safety violations, threats of violence, and scams targeting other users result in immediate permanent removal without warning.

Reporting

Email localuprn@gmail.com. Tell us the profile name, what happened, and when. We look at every report. Child-safety reports come first — put CHILD SAFETY in the subject line.
